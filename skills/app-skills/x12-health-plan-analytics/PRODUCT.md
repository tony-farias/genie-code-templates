# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Health-plan analysts, data engineers, and workshop participants who need to turn raw X12 transactions into governed, queryable healthcare operations data.

## Product Purpose

Provide a repeatable workflow that discovers X12 sources, confirms the intended input, deploys a source-backed parser pipeline, and exposes only curated analytics through a Databricks App and Genie Space. Success means a user can trace every metric to a parsed transaction, inspect data-quality outcomes without seeing raw PHI, and rerun the deployment without visual or data drift.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page decoration, clinical imagery, gratuitous AI motifs, generic card walls, and per-run visual regeneration. The interface must look like an operational analytics product, not a generated prototype.

## Design Principles

- Make provenance and data quality visible without exposing raw X12.
- Keep the primary analytics workflow obvious and fast.
- Use familiar product patterns and restrained visual emphasis.
- Preserve deterministic structure and copy across deployments.
- Treat empty, loading, denied, and failed states as first-class product states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic tables, non-color status cues, reduced-motion compatibility, and responsive layouts down to mobile widths.
